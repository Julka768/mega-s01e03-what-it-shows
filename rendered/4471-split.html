#4471 was merged as one issue: the parser reads dates month first. Read the bodies and the reports describe three different problems. Each one now has its own ticket.

Fixing the parser closes only one of them. The booking-v3 hint and the one-day move stay broken until they get their own fix.

The split

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. 7 April comes out as 4 July.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as told, and the form reads the day first. Same swap, different bug: a parser fix does not reach it.
  • one-day move: the date moves by one day, or differs by time zone (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). No day and month swap at all. The desk already listed these in desk-notes.json, then filed them under the parser.

How a report is placed

  1. One id, one report. The export repeats some reports.
  2. On the desk’s one-day-move list: one-day move.
  3. Came through the form at a tenant that runs booking-v3: hint.
  4. Any other report from a day-first tenant: parser.

The tenant decides, not intake. meta.intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and is not used here.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges reports on the subject line alone. A replacement is proposed in decision 0002. All three problems arrive under the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the rule could never tell them apart. Only the body and the tenant can. A decision is not edited after it is accepted, so a change to that rule needs a new record.
