0002 · Duplicates share a cause, not a subject line

owner Julka768 proposed reviewed 2026-11-05 · 0 days ago

Date: 5 November 2026 · Deciders: Julka768, support desk (to accept) · Replaces: 0001 once accepted

Context

Decision 0001 merges reports when their subject lines match. #4471 shows where that breaks: the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”) arrive for three different problems, and all of them were merged into one ticket marked known. One fix was queued; it reaches only one of the three. See #4471 · three problems, not one.

The cause of a report is already computed in causes.ts, from the tenant (locale and booking form) and the channel. Intake’s tag is a guess and does not decide it.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause, not a subject line:

  1. One id, one report.
  2. Place the report by its tenant and channel (causeOf in causes.ts).
  3. Read the body before merging into a big ticket. A report that does not describe what the ticket describes (for example a one-day move inside a day/month swap ticket) gets its own ticket, even when the subject matches.

Consequences

  • Each ticket holds one problem, and its count means one thing.
  • Triage needs the tenant and a look at the body, so it is slower than subject matching.
  • 0001 stays as it was written. When this record is accepted, 0001 is replaced by it.
